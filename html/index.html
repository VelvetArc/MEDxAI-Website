Our Team

Dr.Sheena Christabel Pravin

CEO

Dr. Sheena Christabel Pravin is a researcher and innovator specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Medical Imaging, Biomedical Signal Processing, and Digital Healthcare Technologies.

Her work focuses on developing AI-driven solutions for cancer pre-screening, medical diagnostics, predictive healthcare analytics, and AI-assisted diagnostic systems. She has expertise in building machine learning and deep learning models for biomedical data analysis, medical imaging, and clinical decision support.

Dr. Sheena is also engaged in in silico drug discovery, leveraging molecular docking, virtual screening, binding affinity prediction, ADMET profiling, and AI-driven molecular intelligence to accelerate therapeutic research. Her interests span precision medicine, oncology, maternal and fetal healthcare, explainable AI, computational biology, and healthcare innovation, with a focus on translating advanced technologies into scalable solutions that improve patient outcomes and healthcare accessibility.

Dr.Reena Monica P

CTO

Dr. Reena Monica P holds a PhD in Nanoelectronics. Her research interest is in the miniaturization of sensors and other computational Integrated Circuits. Her works include - A Carbon Nanotube Field Effect Transistor (CNTFET) based gas sensor fabricated to detect cancer from the Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) in the breath of a person, An efficient filter design for a cardiac pacemaker and the physical design of the complete IC and Quantum computing-driven new material design for Green Electronics.
